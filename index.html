League Rules & Info

Schedule & Timing

  • Warm-up: 7:00 PM
  • Tee-off: 8:00 PM
  • Thursdays · 9-hole rounds

Format

  • 12 total players in the league
  • Maximum 8 players per week (GSPro Local Match limit)
  • RSVP required each week so we know who is playing
  • 12 regular-season weeks + Championship Week on Feb 25
  • 9-hole stroke play (net scores used for points)
  • Best 6 weekly point totals count toward final regular-season standings
  • Players who sit out a week simply have one fewer score — they are not penalized
  • February 25 is Championship Week (separate from regular-season points)

Weekly Spots & RSVP

Because GSPro supports a maximum of 8 players in a single Local Match, only 8 spots are available each Thursday. Please RSVP by Wednesday evening so we can confirm the group. If more than 8 reply, priority goes to players who sat out the previous week, then regular attendees, then first-come.

Handicaps

Starting handicaps: Dan Gudz 17, all others TBD (will be updated as real numbers are provided). Net scores = Gross – Handicap (adjusted for 9 holes). Handicaps can be adjusted during the season based on play. You can edit handicaps directly on the leaderboard.

Scoring

Points awarded each week based on finishing position (net score). Final regular-season standings use each player’s top 6 weekly point totals. Absences do not count as zeros — only the weeks you play matter, and only your best 6 of those count. Championship Week on Feb 25 is separate.

Ties: If two or more players tie, they split the points for the places they occupy. Example: two players tied for 1st each receive (10 + 8) ÷ 2 = 9 points. Three players tied for 1st would each receive (10 + 8 + 6) ÷ 3 = 8 points.
